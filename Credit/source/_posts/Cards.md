People are very divided on the topic of credit cards; one group thinks credit cards are amazing whereas the other group thinks credit cards are evil. Here at Credit College, we take an objective approach to credit card usage and want to educate our readers about the benefits of responsible credit card usage. Below are 10 benefits that credit cards provide:

<ol>
	<li><b>Sign Up Bonus</b>  
        The sign up bonus is indisputably the best part of credit cards because they are essentially free money. Sign up bonuses on no annual fee credit cards range from $100 to $200. Sign up bonuses on some annual fee credit cards are worth between $300 to $600, sometimes more.
    </li>

    <li><b>Cash Back/Points Rewards</b>
            Credit cards usually offer an ongoing cash back or points rewards program that award users around 1% to 3% of their transactions.
    </li>

    <li><b>Building Credit</b>   
            Responsible usage of a credit card builds credit history, improving your credit score which gives access to better loans in the future.
    </li>
    <li> <b>Convenience</b> 
            It is significantly easier to make purchases with a credit card than with cash or check as you do not have to deal with pulling money from an ATM, worry about running out of cash or the cashier giving you the wrong change (or having to carry change at all), spending time writing the check or buying new checks.
    </li>
    <li> <b>Accessibility</b>
        Some transactions can only be made with a credit card, such as online purchases and car rentals. Many restaurants are starting to go “cashless” and only accept card.
    </li>	
    <li><b>Safety</b>
    Losing cash or having a fraudulent charge on your debit card is almost irreversible. Credit card users are never responsible for fraudulent charges and can get replacement credit cards if the card is lost or stolen. 
    </li>
    <li> <b>Expense Tracking</b>
        All purchases made on a credit card are kept on record with details such as date, location and amount. 
    </li>

    <li> <b>Grace Period</b>
	    Credit card bills are due a few weeks after purchases are made which gives your money time to grow if it sits in a high yield checking account.
    </li>
    <li> <b>Extended Warranties</b>
        Most credit cards add 60 days to store warranties, some premium credit cards add up to 2 years of extended warranty.
    </li>
    <li> <b>Car Rental Insurance</b>
        This is exactly what it sounds like.
    </li>
</ol>
